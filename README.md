# Research Compass
> Research lab finder based on student interests and skills
>
> Personal project

## About
Research Compass helps students find research labs at their university that align with their interests and skills. Students are provided with relevant research labs and professors, along with an explanation of why each lab is a good match and additional lab details.

***Goal***

Make the process of finding research labs faster, easier, and more personalized for students


## Progress
✅ Prepared a dataset containing 15+ AI-focused research labs and professors from the University of Michigan Computer Science and Engineering

Using Sentence Transformer (all-MiniLM-L6-v2) to convert student interests and skills into vector embeddings for matching
